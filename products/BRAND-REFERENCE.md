## Image direction

The product cards currently default to a 3:4 portrait crop; other product surfaces use different proportions. Propose consistent 3:4 catalog images, with generous safe space around handles, straps and edges. Live storefront review confirmed this portrait catalog direction; keep safe space for gallery crops. Use cream/stone surroundings and controlled, soft shadows. Preserve supplier photos and inspect every candidate beside the source. Do not invent hidden views, extra compartments, stitching, branding, hardware, colorways, materials or accessories. Branded backgrounds must not imply that an accessory is included.

## Content and theme compatibility

Use specific, concise product names and clear, understated English. Organize descriptions into a short introduction, confirmed features and verified specifications. Do not add claims such as full-grain leather, water resistance, manufacturing origin, delivery promises or warranties without reliable product/store evidence. Supplier facts that conflict or are unclear remain unresolved rather than being polished into claims.

The theme uses native product descriptions, variants/prices and option names Color/Colour for swatches. Product accordions read custom.material, custom.dimensions and custom.care. Care guidance needs material-specific confirmation. Choose existing types, collections, tags and templates from the live Shopify store after access is authorized. Do not create fake reviews, discounts, compare-at prices or stock counts. Supplier costs are not approved retail prices.
