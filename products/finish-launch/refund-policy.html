30-day returns

You may request a change-of-mind return within 30 days after your order is delivered. Tell us your order number and the items you want to return using veyneatelier@gmail.com or our contact form. Send the items back within 14 days after notifying us.

Condition of returned items

Please return items in the condition received, with accessories and original packaging where available. You may inspect an item as you would in a shop. Where the law permits, a deduction may apply for loss in value caused by handling beyond what is reasonably necessary to inspect it. Missing packaging does not remove your statutory rights.

Return postage and instructions

For a change of mind, you are responsible for return postage. We do not charge a restocking fee. Contact us for the correct return address and instructions before posting; the address on the delivery parcel may not be a returns address. Keep proof of postage and use a service appropriate to the item's value.

Damaged, faulty or incorrect items

Contact us as soon as possible with your order number and, where available, photographs of the issue. We will arrange an appropriate remedy and cover necessary return postage for faulty, damaged or incorrect items. You do not have to buy another item to obtain a remedy. The 30-day change-of-mind window does not limit your statutory rights.

Refunds

Refunds are made to the original payment method within 14 days after we receive the returned goods or evidence that they were sent back, whichever is earlier where applicable. Your bank or payment provider may take additional time to display the refund. Standard outbound delivery is refunded when required by applicable cancellation rights; any extra amount paid for a premium delivery service is not included unless required by law.

Cancellation and exchanges

Contact us promptly if you want to cancel before dispatch. Once an order has been dispatched, the return process applies. For an exchange, contact us so we can confirm availability and the appropriate return process.

Your rights

This voluntary policy is additional to your legal rights. UK customers retain their distance-selling cancellation rights, including notifying us within 14 days after delivery and a further 14 days to return the goods. Sale items have the same statutory protections. Nothing in this policy excludes rights relating to faulty, misdescribed or otherwise non-conforming goods.
