Shipping

Our current bags are dispatched from China through our fulfillment partner. Enter your delivery address at checkout to see the destinations, shipping services and charges available for your order. If no service is offered for your address, please contact us before ordering.

Processing and delivery estimates

Allow approximately 2–5 days for order processing before dispatch. This is an estimate based on our fulfillment partner's general guidance; products sourced from supplier stock may require additional preparation. We will contact you if a significant delay affects your order.

For US and UK orders using our standard tracked service, current carrier estimates are approximately 4–9 days in transit after dispatch, in addition to processing. These are estimates rather than guaranteed delivery dates. Customs checks, local holidays, remote-area delivery and carrier disruption may extend the time. Contact us before ordering if you need delivery by a particular date.

Charges and multiple items

Standard tracked shipping for bags on our CJ fulfillment profile is US $19.95 per order to the United States and US $12.95 per order to the United Kingdom, charged in USD. Morocco rates and any services for other items are shown at checkout. Orders containing items from different shipping profiles may have combined charges; review the final total before payment.

Items may arrive in separate parcels; we will provide available tracking information for each shipment.

Tracking, addresses and delivery problems

Tracking information is sent when it becomes available after dispatch. Check your address carefully before paying. If you spot an address error, contact us promptly; changes may not be possible after dispatch. If tracking stops updating or delivery is overdue, contact us and we will investigate with the fulfillment partner or carrier.

Taxes and import charges

Taxes collected by the store are shown at checkout. Depending on the destination and delivery arrangement, customs may assess import charges that are not collected at checkout. Contact us before ordering if you need clarification; this page does not promise duty-free delivery.

Contact

For delivery or return questions, use veyneatelier@gmail.com or our contact form. Include your order number if you have already ordered.
